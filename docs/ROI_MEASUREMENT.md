# ROI measurement

Measure before and after automation for every pilot. Do not report revenue as AI-generated without an auditable connection between the action and the commercial outcome.

## Core measures
- Median first-response time
- Lead-to-meeting rate
- Quote creation rate
- Quote-to-paid conversion rate
- Collected revenue
- Follow-up completion rate
- Manual hours avoided
- Approval rate, rejection rate, expiry rate, and execution failures

## Attribution
An outcome is `direct` only when the source lead, opportunity, approved action, provider receipt, and payment or signed contract can be linked. Outcomes without that chain are `assisted` or `unattributed`.

## Pilot success gate
After 30 days, compare the pilot to its documented baseline. The product passes only if it improves at least one agreed commercial metric without a material safety incident or unresolved audit gap.
