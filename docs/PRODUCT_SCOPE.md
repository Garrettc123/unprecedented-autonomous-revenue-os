# Governed Lead-to-Cash Beta

This beta turns the platform into a controlled revenue workflow for a single design-partner business.

## Customer promise
Improve speed-to-lead, follow-up consistency, and visibility into lead-to-cash performance while requiring approval for material, customer-facing, financial, or irreversible actions.

## Scope
1. Ingest a lead from one approved connector.
2. Create or match a customer and opportunity.
3. Create an AI action proposal with evidence and expected value.
4. Evaluate policy.
5. Auto-execute only low-risk internal actions; route all other actions to approval.
6. Execute approved actions exactly once.
7. Persist provider receipts and outcome events.
8. Report influenced revenue only when linked to source evidence.

## Not in beta
No autonomous price changes, money movement, refunds, contract signatures, public posting, data deletion, production deployments, hiring decisions, or bulk outreach.
