# Compliance & Legal Guardrails

This system is designed for legitimate business only.

## Required Before Any Outreach
- [ ] Physical business address in every email footer
- [ ] One-click unsubscribe that works within 10 days (CAN-SPAM)
- [ ] Honest subject lines (no deceptive "Re:" or "Fw:")
- [ ] Clear identification of sender
- [ ] GDPR lawful basis documented (legitimate interest for B2B + soft opt-in where applicable)

## Stripe
- All payments processed through Stripe
- No manual "invoice me later" workarounds that skip tax reporting
- Automatic tax calculation enabled where required

## Data
- Only public or customer-provided data
- 72-hour deletion SLA on request
- Immutable audit log of every agent decision

## Prohibited
- Fake scarcity or countdown timers that reset
- Claims of guaranteed results without evidence
- Scraping private data or violating robots.txt / ToS of source sites
- Any activity that would fail a SOC 2 Type II audit

Violations of this document are considered system defects and must be fixed immediately.
